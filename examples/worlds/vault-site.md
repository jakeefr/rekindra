# Vault site study

`vault-site.scene.json` preserves the retained native Hermes site/pacing revision, with reference asset IDs replaced by sequential 32-character hexadecimal placeholders. Remap these to locally imported matching source photographs before creating a world. Read each reference's observed evidence before remapping; do not substitute unrelated images and retain the old observations. Source images are not bundled.

This is an authored architectural interpretation, not recovered real-world geometry or a mandatory generator template. The source material is night-time Quebec architecture; the opening vault and lantern mechanism are inventions. It evolved through directed development and native review loops, not one unattended prompt.

The original Rekindra renderer supplies geometry, shared assembly motion, materials, four static point lights and offline browser/video output. No external creative engine is required. Text is absent from the scene; labels on review contact sheets are diagnostic only.

The retained site has an extended approach, open entrance and surrounding terrain. Its fixed-camera diagnostic detects activity earlier and later than the previous baseline. These measurements do not establish artistic quality or exclusively geometric motion. The study remains sparse, with matte-looking surfaces and a slowly changing ending; it is not a finished showcase.
